    Assignment: Exploring AI for Environmental Science

    Artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly important in environmental science, from data analysis and coding to modeling, visualization, remote sensing, literature review, and scientific communication. At the same time, AI is developing so quickly that it is difficult for any of us to keep up with all of the new tools and methods.

    I am not an AI expert myself, so rather than trying to teach you AI entirely from my own perspective, I would like us to learn from some of the leaders in the field. In particular, Andrew Ng has been one of the most influential educators in artificial intelligence and machine learning, and DeepLearning.AI offers a number of high-quality courses taught by him and other experts.

    For this assignment, you will choose the AI topics that are most relevant to your own interests and spend one week exploring them. The goal is not to become an AI expert in a few days, but to gain a practical understanding of what current AI tools can do and to begin thinking about how they might be useful in environmental science.

    Your Task

    Visit the DeepLearning.AI course catalog.

    Choose one course or a combination of short courses from the recommended list below. Spend approximately 3–5 hours during this week learning about AI.

    You do not need to complete a long course in its entirety. For longer courses, complete the portions that are most relevant to your interests.

    Recommended Courses

    1. AI Prompting for Everyone — Andrew Ng

    Level: Beginner
    Length: ~3 hours

    Learn how to communicate effectively with modern AI systems, find information, conduct research, brainstorm, write, analyze data, and work with multimedia and code.

    Recommended for: Everyone, particularly students who want to use AI as a research and analysis assistant.

    2. Generative AI for Everyone — Andrew Ng

    Level: Beginner
    Length: ~5 hours

    Learn what generative AI and large language models are, what they can and cannot do, and how they can be used responsibly.

    Recommended for: Students who want a broad understanding of modern generative AI without much programming.

    3. AI for Everyone — Andrew Ng

    Level: Beginner
    Length: ~7 hours

    A broader introduction to artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, AI project workflows, and the societal impacts of AI.

    Recommended for: Students who have little previous exposure to AI and want to understand the basic concepts behind it.

    4. AI for Good — Robert Monarch

    Level: Beginner
    Full program: ~38 hours

    Explore how AI and machine learning can be applied to real-world societal and environmental problems. Topics include:

    • Air-quality monitoring
    • Climate change and wind-energy modeling
    • Biodiversity monitoring
    • Disaster management and damage assessment

    Recommended for: Environmental science students who want to see how AI can be applied directly to environmental and sustainability problems.

    For this assignment, you are not expected to complete the full program. Select approximately 3–5 hours of material from the topics most relevant to your interests.

    5. ChatGPT Prompt Engineering for Developers — Isa Fulford & Andrew Ng

    Level: Beginner
    Length: ~1.5 hours

    Learn systematic approaches for prompting large language models to summarize, extract information, transform text, classify information, and build simple AI-powered workflows.

    Recommended for: Students interested in using AI more systematically for research, data analysis, or automated workflows.

    Because this course is relatively short, combine it with another short course if needed to reach approximately 3 hours.

    6. Build with Andrew — Andrew Ng

    Level: Beginner
    Length: ~1 hour

    Learn how AI can help you build a simple working application even without extensive programming experience.

    Recommended for: Students interested in developing simple environmental tools, interactive applications, calculators, dashboards, or educational tools.

    Because this course is short, combine it with another course.

    7. Agentic AI — Andrew Ng

    Level: Intermediate
    Length: ~8 hours

    Learn how AI systems can perform multi-step tasks using reflection, tool use, planning, and multi-agent workflows.

    Recommended for: Students who want to explore more advanced AI applications, such as automated environmental data analysis or research workflows.

    For this assignment, complete approximately 3–5 hours of the course.

    What to Submit

    Use the provided Word template and submit a short reflection of approximately 1–2 pages covering the following four items.

    1. Course(s) Completed

    Provide:

    • Course title(s)
    • Instructor(s)
    • Modules or sections completed
    • Approximately how much time you spent

    2. Three Things You Learned

    Briefly describe three concepts, techniques, or tools that were new or particularly useful to you.

    3. Environmental Science Application

    Describe one specific environmental science problem where something you learned could be useful.

    Possible examples include:

    • Air-quality analysis
    • Climate or weather data
    • Water-quality monitoring
    • Remote sensing and satellite imagery
    • Ecological or biodiversity data
    • Environmental health
    • Environmental modeling
    • Pollution source identification
    • Environmental literature review
    • Data visualization and communication

    4. Complete a Course Assignment or Exercise

    Complete at least one assignment, quiz, lab, code exercise, project, or other hands-on activity associated with the module(s) you studied.

    Briefly report:

    • Which assignment or exercise you completed
    • What the task asked you to do
    • What you learned from completing it

    Some DeepLearning.AI graded assignments require a Pro/paid subscription. You are not required to pay for this assignment. If the graded activity in your chosen module is not freely accessible, complete an available code example, lab demonstration, hands-on activity, or similar exercise from the module instead.

    Grading

    This assignment is graded primarily on engagement and thoughtful reflection, not on previous programming or AI experience.

    Component Weight
    Completion of AI learning activities 30%
    Three key lessons learned 20%
    Environmental science application 20%
    Completion and discussion of a course assignment/exercise 30%

    The purpose of this assignment is not to make you an AI expert in one week. The goal is to become familiar with current AI capabilities and begin thinking about how these tools can be used thoughtfully in environmental science.
